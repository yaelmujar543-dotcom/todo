A Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (BSCS) is a 4-year undergraduate program focused on the mathematical and algorithmic foundations of computing, software engineering, and hardware-software systems

Information Systems (IS) course or degree program blends technology, business management, and human processes to design and manage solutions that help organizations achieve their goals.

ACT

An Associate in Computer Technology (ACT) is a two-year non-degree technical-vocational program that awards a diploma in computer technology and serves as a ladderized stepping stone toward a full bachelor's degree.
